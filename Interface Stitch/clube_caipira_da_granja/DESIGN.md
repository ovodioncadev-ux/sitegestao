## Brand & Style

### Personality & Atmosphere
This design system expresses a refined, tactile, and agrarian spirit grounded in artisanal farming, ethical livestock management, and countryside provenance. It rejects the cold, sterile conventions of tech platforms—such as synthetic blues, stark digital whites, hyper-saturated purples, and artificial glassmorphic blurs—in favor of warmth, quiet nobility, and organic tangibility. The UI evokes the morning sun on rural pastureland, fresh eggshells, and rich fertile soil.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
The target audience comprises conscious consumers, culinary enthusiasts, and families seeking verifiable food traceability, animal welfare (pasture-raised, cage-free), and premium rural freshness. The interface must evoke:
- **Serenity & Trust:** Grounded structural warmth through earthy foundations without clinical aloofness.
- **Sensory Freshness:** Natural vibrance keyed strictly around rich egg-yolk golden accents.
- **Artisanal Care:** Tangible, card-based surfaces evoking fine unbleached paper, linen, and clay rather than plastic hardware.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Language
The aesthetic merges **Contemporary Warm Minimalism** with **Tactile/Artisanal Skeuomorphic Subtlety**:
- **Surfaces:** Flat, ungraduated alabaster-cream planes layered with micro-contrast instead of harsh division.
- **Borders:** Thin, organic earth-pigment outlines reminiscent of botanical stationery.
- **Accents:** High-density golden yolk highlights and olive-green flora tokens used with strict economy.

## Colors

### Surface & Canvas Architecture
- **Base Canvas (`#FDFBF7`):** Light, warm alabaster cream. Replaces pure `#FFFFFF` across all outer canvases and viewports to eliminate screen glare and evoke natural eggshell grain.
- **Container Base (`#F9F5EF`):** Subtle linen cream for nested section groupings and relaxed reading zones.
- **Container Elevated (`#F4EADE`):** Warm butter cream utilized for accent cards, table headers, and structural provenance highlights.
- **Container Lowest (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved exclusively for elevated primary foreground cards requiring crisp contrast against the warm cream canvas.

### Accent & Interaction
- **Primary / Dourado-Gema (`#E67E22`):** Rich caipira egg yolk orange-gold. Reserved exclusively for primary conversion targets, critical calls-to-action (CTAs), key value badges, and active state indicators.
- **Primary Hover (`#D35400`):** Deep roasted amber providing tactile feedback on active hover states.
- **Secondary / Verde-Oliva (`#556B2F`):** Muted botanical olive-green representing pastured fields, sustainability certifications, and humane breeding credentials.
- **Tertiary / Âmbar Solar (`#F39C12`):** Auxiliary gold for decorative accents, freshness ratings, and secondary visual markers.

### Typography & Linework
- **Primary Earth (`#2B1E1A`):** Deep roasted espresso and fertile topsoil. The sole color token for primary titles, structural frames, heavy borders, and high-emphasis interface elements.
- **Muted Earth (`#5C4942`):** Burnt clay for long-form running body text, field hints, and supportive captions.
- **Subtle Earth / Border (`rgba(43, 30, 26, 0.08)` / `#E2D7C8`):** Delicate linear divisions and structural separators.

### Functional Status Tokens
- **Success (`#2D6A4F` / Container `#E8F4EC`):** Deep forest green. Discretely applied to delivery route validation, subscriber status badges, and order confirmations.
- **Error / Warning Alert (`#9A3412` / Container `#FDF0EB`):** Burnt terracotta clay. Serious, grounded, and legible; avoids synthetic fluorescent reds.
- **WhatsApp Direct (`#25D366`):** Dedicated exclusively to immediate concierge support triggers.

## Typography

### Structural Triad
1. **Display & Headings (Outfit):** High-personality, geometric sans with generous curved strokes that reflect the organic geometry of eggs. Strict compact leading (`1.15` to `1.2`) and negative tracking prevent typographic fragmentation across Portuguese headings.
2. **Interface & Narrative (Archivo):** Uncompromising, robust grotesque engineered for continuous legibility across screen sizes. Generous line-height (`1.5` to `1.55`) ensures fatigue-free reading of artisanal agricultural practices and plan terms.
3. **Operational & Tabular (IBM Plex Mono):** Precise monospaced numerals reserved strictly for batch identifiers, farm harvest timestamps, prices (`R$ 164`), quantity step indicators, and route postcodes.

### Hierarchy & Composition Rules
- **No Intersystem Swapping:** Never apply Outfit to body narrative or Archivo to standalone big numerical pricing figures.
- **Uppercase Restraint:** Uppercase transforms are limited to `label-caps` for farm badges, subscription tiers, and provenance tags, with tracking expanded to `+0.08em` for optical balance.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Structure & Grid
- **Global Container Constraint:** Centered layout capped at a maximum width of `1200px`.
- **Desktop Grid (> 1024px):** 12-column responsive grid with `24px` (`1.5rem`) gutters and `24px` outer canvas margins.
- **Tablet Grid (640px - 1023px):** 6-column grid with `20px` gutters and `20px` margins.
- **Mobile Grid (< 640px):** Single-column layout with `16px` (`1rem`) lateral margins. Elements stack vertically, and primary action buttons expand to full container width.

### Rhythm & Whitespace Rules
- **Section Interval:** High macro-spacing rhythm of `80px` to `96px` (`space-4xl`) between vertical landing blocks to maintain a relaxed, premium pace.
- **Component Padding Scale:** Small cards and input elements use `space-md` (`1rem`) padding; primary product cards and feature modules use `space-xl` (`2rem`) padding.
- **Vertical Alignment:** Editorial content features consistent left-alignment on both desktop and mobile, maintaining an anchored vertical baseline.

## Elevation & Depth

### Tonal Stratification & Warm Shadows
Visual hierarchy is conveyed through tonal layering combined with faint, warm-tinted ambient shadows. Pure black drop-shadows are strictly prohibited.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#FDFBF7` baseline with zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Inset / Passive Cards):** `#F9F5EF` surfaces bordered by `1px` solid `rgba(43, 30, 26, 0.08)` (`#E2D7C8`). Shadows are omitted or kept to ambient diffusions of `0 2px 8px rgba(43, 30, 26, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Foreground Cards):** `#FFFFFF` cards resting on `#FDFBF7` backgrounds, bordered with `1px` subtle earth-line, elevated by warm ambient drop shadows: `0 10px 30px rgba(43, 30, 26, 0.05)`.
- **Level 3 (Featured Plan & Prominent Actions):** Primary interactive targets and featured subscription cards utilize golden-tinted diffusion: `0 15px 45px rgba(230, 126, 34, 0.16)`.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Overlays):** Surface `#FFFFFF` over an earth-tinted backdrop scrim (`rgba(43, 30, 26, 0.45)`) combined with `0 25px 60px rgba(43, 30, 26, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

### Curvature Tokens
- **Standard Curvature (`rounded` / 0.5rem):** Form utility controls, secondary inputs, and tabular data cards.
- **Large Curvature (`rounded-lg` / 1.0rem):** Input text fields, checkout step pills, and notification snackbars.
- **Extra Large Curvature (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem to 2.0rem):** Primary feature cards, subscription containers, farm manifesto images, and bottom drawers.
- **Pill (`rounded-full`):** Primary action buttons, status chips, freshness indicators, and quantity counters.
